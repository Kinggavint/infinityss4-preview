Helping children and families feel grounded when everything else feels uncertain

Our mission is to support families with children facing hardship caused by the critical illness or loss of a parent to lessen the burden and aid prosperity for families in our community in honor of Susanna May.

1 in 12 children in the United States will lose a parent before the age of 18,* and more than half of families facing a critical illness report being financially unprepared.

*National Alliance for Children's Grief (NACG)

Donate

When a parent gets sick, we provide financial support so children can maintain their usual routine

Our support is organized around four pillars: Health & Wellness, Childhood Prosperity, Education, and Essential Needs, designed to address the full picture of the challenges a family may be facing.

Health & Wellness

Susanna found that many medical expenses were not covered by traditional insurance and the extensive financial burden was unexpected. Expenses supported are insurance deductibles, out of pocket medical expenses, alternative medicine, therapy, preventative education, counseling, prosthetics, palliative and hospice care.

Caring for the whole person

  • Medical Expenses
  • Alternative Medicine
  • Therapy
  • Preventative Education
  • Counseling
  • Prosthetics
  • Palliative & Hospice Care

Child Prosperity

Susanna exhibited compassion in caring and providing for children in need in our community. We support childhood prosperity by offering support to maintain a balance for normalcy by providing day-to-day supplies, clothing, educational tools, and fees for extra-curricular activities. Children also may need therapy/counseling, grief support, emotional support, and diversion. To counterbalance stress and worry, we provide care packages and organize supportive notes or activities to support and care for children.

Compassion in Caring and Providing for Children in Need in Our Community

  • Therapy/Counseling
  • Grief Support
  • Diversion
  • Supplies and Educational Tools
  • Extra-Curricular Activities
  • Care Packages

Essential Needs

In the case of critical illness or tragedy, income may be reduced or stretched with additional expenses and a decrease in their ability to work. The organization offers resources for housing support, food and nutrition, utilities, cleaning services, or assistance to find additional resources for social services or assistance with housing/utilities, transportation, caregiver support, legal / financial services and funeral service expenses.

Leading Assistance with Essential needs of Families Impacted by Illness of Patient

  • Housing & Utilities Support
  • Food & Nutrition
  • Cleaning Services
  • Caregiver & Transportation
  • Financial & Legal Services
  • Funeral Expenses

Education

Susanna attended Catholic schools as a child. She and her husband believed in the quality of private education and the sense of community that came with it. We are committed to supporting educational needs including, tuition, tutoring, organized traditional/non-traditional courses, preschool, day care, professional services, technology and supplies that a child may need for educational purposes. Scholarships may be offered at a later date to help with educational needs.

Providing Educational Needs

  • Tutoring
  • Preschool/Day Care
  • Technology & Supplies
  • Professional Services
  • Scholarships

Board of Directors

We are proud to be a Kansas City organization serving Kansas City families.

The Board of Directors evaluates blind requests from the Application Committee from individuals that have met the criteria outlined by the organization to assure a substantial impact in the community and manages all fundraising efforts. The Board is a volunteer board; no payment is received for being on the board. Board members have voting privileges during Board meetings.

The Board is made up of the following individuals committed to making impactful donations for families with children that have a parent facing a life altering illness or death. The Board meets regularly to ensure we are continually making progress in improving the quality of life for children in our community.

FAQs

Who can apply for financial assistance through Infinity Susanna Smiles?

Families with children at home who are facing hardship caused by the critical illness or loss of a parent may apply.

How long does the process take?

The process typically takes 2 to 4 weeks.

How do I know funds were received by the family?

After an application is received, a volunteer from our review board contacts the applicant. The applicant sends the bills to be covered, and the board reviews and approves the request. The applicant is then told the result and when the bills have been paid.

Is my donation to Infinity Susanna Smiles tax-deductible?

Yes. Your donation is tax-deductible, limited to the amount that exceeds the fair market value of any goods or services provided in return.

Does Infinity Susanna Smiles accept checks?

Yes. Please make checks payable to Infinity Susanna Smiles and mail them to Infinity Susanna Smiles Inc., PO Box 1139, Lee’s Summit MO 64063.

Is Infinity Susanna Smiles a 501c3?

Yes. Infinity Susanna Smiles is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Our EIN is 92-1517774.

Our support is not one-size-fits-all

We work with each family to understand where the need is greatest and direct resources accordingly.
