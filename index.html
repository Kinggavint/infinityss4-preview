The Mission and Purpose of Infinity Susanna Smiles

Our mission is to support families with children facing hardship caused by the critical illness or loss of a parent to lessen the burden and aid prosperity for families in our community in honor of Susanna May.

Our purpose is not just to provide financial assistance to families but to ensure that every individual and family we touch can move towards a more fulfilled and prosperous life, feeling whole and supported in their journey.

Immediate relief through four key pillars

When a parent passes away, the emotional weight of loss and the weight of financial pressure often arrive at the same time. Infinity Susanna Smiles steps in during that exact window so families don't have to face the hardest part alone.

Our support covers real, everyday costs and is organized around four pillars: Health & Wellness, Childhood Prosperity, Education, and Essential Needs, designed to address the full picture of the challenges a family may be facing.

Health & Wellness

We cover medical expenses like therapy, prosthetics, alternative medicine, palliative & hospice care, and more

Child Prosperity

Compassionate care through grief support, care packages, extra-curricular activities, diversion, and more

Essential Needs

We provide assistance with essentials like food & nutrition, housing & utilities, transportation, and more

Education

Our support funds tuition, day care, scholarships, technology & education supplies, tutoring, and more

How It Works

With infinite compassion, we help families stay financially intact

In 2024/2025, Infinity Susanna Smiles provided over $350,000 in direct support to families across the Kansas City Metro and surrounding communities.

  1. Step 1

    Submit an application for assistance

    Applications can be submitted by anyone who knows a family in need: a friend, neighbor, family member, or healthcare provider.

  2. Step 2

    Our selection committee reviews applications

    Every application is reviewed confidentially by a dedicated selection committee, protecting each family’s dignity throughout the process.

  3. Step 3

    We pay vendors directly

    Every dollar is paid directly to vendors and service providers, ensuring complete accountability and transparency from donation to impact.

FAQs

Who can apply for financial assistance through Infinity Susanna Smiles?

Families with children at home who are facing hardship caused by the critical illness or loss of a parent may apply.

How long does the process take?

The process typically takes 2 to 4 weeks.

How do I know funds were received by the family?

After an application is received, a volunteer from our review board contacts the applicant. The applicant sends the bills to be covered, and the board reviews and approves the request. The applicant is then told the result and when the bills have been paid.

Is my donation to Infinity Susanna Smiles tax-deductible?

Yes. Your donation is tax-deductible, limited to the amount that exceeds the fair market value of any goods or services provided in return.

Does Infinity Susanna Smiles accept checks?

Yes. Please make checks payable to Infinity Susanna Smiles and mail them to Infinity Susanna Smiles Inc., PO Box 1139, Lee’s Summit MO 64063.

Is Infinity Susanna Smiles a 501c3?

Yes. Infinity Susanna Smiles is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Our EIN is 92-1517774.
