Infinity - Susanna Smiles runs entirely on the dedication of its volunteers

Whether you join the board, serve on the selection committee, or lend your time at an event, there is a place for you here.

Infinity - Susanna Smiles is a volunteer-run organization with no paid employees, driven by passionate individuals committed to making a difference.

Ways to Help

If you need assistance from the organization, please fill out our application on the “Get Help” Page and tell us how we can best help you.

Donations are needed to ensure the organization can help as many people as possible. Your generosity and kindness are welcomed in cash, in-kind donations for items or professional services, event sponsors, corporate donations etc. Every dollar, no matter how big or small, has an impact in helping those in need. If this organization’s mission and purpose speaks to you, please donate or contact us if you can offer an in-kind donation or provide much needed services as outlined in our Pillars.

If you would like to get involved with the organization, please fill out the Volunteer form on our Donate page if you would like to contact our Board of Directors, please fill out form below, or if you want to donate now

Volunteer interest

Some of the most meaningful things you can do for Infinity - Susanna Smiles cost nothing at all. Sharing our story, referring a family in need, or simply telling someone what we do plants a seed that can change a family's life.

Follow Our Social Media

Infinity - Susanna Smiles is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit offering financial support to families with children at home in the Kansas City Metro and surrounding communities when a parent is facing or has passed away from cancer, a critical illness, or a life-altering medical event.
