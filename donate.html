Donations go directly to families in need

Infinity - Susanna Smiles helps families stay financially intact by covering real, everyday costs: mortgage and rent payments, medical bills, utilities, tuition, tutoring, therapy, grief support and more.

Donations provide support across four core pillars designed to address the full picture of the challenges a family may be facing: Health & Wellness, Childhood Prosperity, Education, and Essential Needs.

Donate

Losing a parent is a profoundly devastating and life-altering experience, often accompanied by significant financial burdens.

At Infinity - Susanna Smiles, we offer financial grants to assist families in the greater Kansas City area during these trying times. These funds offer immediate relief, empowering families to allocate support where it's most urgently needed. Our goal is to help as many families as possible with the donations we receive.

Families go through so much financial and emotional stress while dealing with a serious or terminal illness. These contributions can go toward expenses that would help the family, including their mortgage, utilities, daycare, medical treatments, etc. All funds will be sent directly from Infinity to the vendor. We are seeking contributions to help us with our mission to assist as many families as possible. Susanna was dedicated to helping friends, family and community and we are committed to continuing her legacy of positivity, loving and giving to others.

Donate

Infinity - Susanna Smiles is 100% volunteer-run, with nearly every dollar donated going directly to a real family's real bills, made possible by the generous individuals, businesses, and community partners across the Kansas City Metro and surrounding communities.

There is no paid staff at Infinity - Susanna Smiles. Every person who powers this organization, from the board to the selection committee to our event volunteers, gives their time because they believe in this mission.

Your donation stays local and makes an immediate difference close to home.

We have built meaningful relationships with community organizations, local hospitals, and corporate partners who share our belief that Kansas City takes care of its own.
