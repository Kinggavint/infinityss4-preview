We cover real, everyday costs

Infinity Susanna Smiles is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit offering financial support to families in the Kansas City Metro and surrounding communities when a parent is facing or has passed away from cancer, a critical illness, or a life-altering medical event.

Our support is organized around four pillars: Health & Wellness, Childhood Prosperity, Education, and Essential Needs, designed to address the full picture of the challenges a family may be facing.

When a family is in crisis, immediate relief is crucial.

Infinity Susanna Smiles is built for speed and simplicity, providing direct financial relief without long wait periods, excessive paperwork, or bureaucratic hurdles standing between families and the help they need.

Process

  1. Apply for support

    Applications can be submitted by anyone who knows a family in need: a friend, neighbor, family member, or healthcare provider.

  2. Application is reviewed

    Every application is reviewed confidentially by a dedicated selection committee, protecting each family's dignity throughout the process.

  3. Assistance is offered

    Every dollar is paid directly to vendors and service providers, ensuring complete accountability and transparency from donation to impact.
